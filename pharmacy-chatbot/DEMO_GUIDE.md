# 🎯 Demo Guide - PharmaCare AI Assistant

This guide will help you explore all the features of the pharmacy chatbot demo.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Open the Application**: Visit `http://localhost:8080` in your browser
2. **Welcome Message**: The AI assistant will greet you with available services
3. **Start Chatting**: Type your questions or click quick action buttons

## 🛒 Shopping Features

### Add Items to Cart
1. Ask about symptoms (e.g., "I have a headache")
2. View recommended products in the right panel
3. Click "Add to Cart" on any product
4. Notice the cart count update in the top-right

### Manage Cart
1. Click the shopping cart icon (top-right)
2. View all items in the sidebar
3. Adjust quantities using +/- buttons
4. Remove items with the trash icon
5. See real-time price calculations

### Checkout Process
1. Click "Proceed to Checkout" in the cart
2. Fill out the customer information form
3. Enter payment details (demo - use fake data)
4. Review your order summary
5. Click "Place Order" to complete
